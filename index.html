About the project

Our goals & vision

We believe education becomes truly transformative when it crosses borders.

  • Cross-border cooperation with EU schools of similar curriculum scope
  • English language training for non-language teachers at various levels
  • Student group mobilities fostering Spanish, German and English language skills
  • Democratic thinking, active citizenship and critical thinking development
  • Inclusion — integrating students with special needs and fewer opportunities
  • Digital skills for language teachers — AI tools, IT in education, online safety
  • Methodological innovation: CLIL, PBL and modern teaching approaches
  • Involving parents and the wider school community

Group Mobility · Levice, Slovakia

Valencia meets Levice

15–19 December 2025 · GAV Levice · 🇪🇸 🇸🇰

Students from IES El Grao Valencia arrived in Levice just before Christmas — festive lights, Christmas market and snow in Banská Štiavnica.

Day 1 — Christmas market

Levice Christmas market on Sunday evening, then bar Trip for pizza, fries and first cross-language conversations.

Day 2 — School & Levice Castle

Ice-breaking games, fake news lesson with Ms. Bieliková, Spanish Christmas culture class, then a city sightseeing game to Levice Castle.

Day 3 — Ice skating & Banská Štiavnica

First time on skates for many Spanish students. Snowy Banská Štiavnica and the Cesta Časom museum.

Day 4 — Bratislava

Sightseeing and the iconic group photo in front of the Slovak Parliament with three national flags.

Democracy across the curriculum

English and Philosophy lessons on democracy and EU by Ms. Riecka and Ms. Belicova throughout the week.

Group Mobility · Levice, Slovakia

Germany comes to Levice

2–6 March 2026 · GAV Levice · 🇩🇪 🇸🇰

Students from IGS Schaumburg in Stadthagen arrived in Levice for a full week of cultural exchange and school activities.

Monday — City game, Levice Castle & Koliba dinner

EU lessons in English and German, city riddle game, Levice Castle, synagogue and town hall. Dinner at Koliba u Šidla — bryndzóve halušky and Kofola. Some students also visited Esztergom Cathedral in Hungary.

Tuesday — Migration workshop, picnic & go-karting

IOM migration role-play workshop, Slovak food picnic in the castle park, go-karting in Seč and motorbike riding.

Wednesday — EU lessons & laser arena in Nitra

Slovak students delivered EU presentations in German. Kahoot on democracy, then Nitra for laser arena and sushi.

Thursday — Bratislava

Old town sightseeing, the Čumil statue, 1602 witch burning plaque, tourist train, Eurovea and bar Trip.

Impact

Project outcomes

What this project has achieved and what it is building toward — for students, teachers, the school and the wider community.

🌐
International competence
Students developed real cross-cultural communication skills, formed lasting friendships across five countries and gained first-hand experience of living and studying in a different European culture.
🗣️
Language development
Meaningful improvement in English, Spanish and German through authentic communication in mobility contexts. Non-language teachers also strengthened their English for classroom use.
🗃️
Democratic values & critical thinking
Students across all mobility schools engaged with workshops on EU citizenship, fake news, the Holocaust and human rights — building informed, reflective young Europeans.
🤝
Inclusion & diversity
The project actively integrated students with special needs and from fewer-opportunity backgrounds into all mobility activities, creating a genuinely inclusive European learning community.
💻
Teacher professional development
Language and non-language teachers gained new skills in digital tools, AI in education, CLIL and PBL methodologies, directly improving the quality of teaching at GAV Levice.
🏢
Institutional capacity
GAV Levice has strengthened its position as an internationally active school, building a network of European partner institutions and a model of accredited Erasmus+ mobility for other Slovak schools.
